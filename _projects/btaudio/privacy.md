**Effective date:** October 28, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the BTAudio companion app for Android (the "App"), published by Nollstead Studio, handles information. The App is a configuration tool for the BTAudio device.

## Summary

The App does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

## What the App does

The App uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to find a nearby BTAudio device, connect to it, and read and change the device's audio settings, such as volume, input and equalizer. All communication happens directly between your phone and your BTAudio device.

## Information we collect

None. The App does not collect personal information, device identifiers, usage data, crash reports or location data. Nothing from the App is sent to Nollstead Studio or to any third party.

## Permissions the App requests

- **Nearby devices (Bluetooth scan and connect):** Required to find and communicate with your BTAudio device.
- **Location (Android 10 and 11 only):** Those versions of Android require the location permission in order to scan for Bluetooth devices. The App does not read, record or use your location.

The App uses these permissions only while you are using it to connect to your device.

## Data stored on your device

The App does not keep an account or profile. Your audio settings are stored on the BTAudio device itself, not in the App or on any server.

## Third-party services and links

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services. The About screen contains links to websites, such as nollsteadstudio.com. If you open a link, it loads in your browser and that website's own privacy policy applies.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to nollsteadstudio@gmail.com.
